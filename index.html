Section 01 • Master Concepts

1. Introduction: Fundamentals of the Bipolar Junction Transistor

A Bipolar Junction Transistor (BJT) is a three-terminal active semiconductor device where a small control current at the Base regulates a substantially larger current between Collector and Emitter.

📖 Foundational Principles & Definitions

Semiconductor Doping

Adding controlled impurities (pentavalent donors for N-type, trivalent acceptors for P-type) into pure silicon lattice.

Bipolar Charge Conduction

Both electrons (e⁻) and holes (h⁺) contribute to total current conduction, distinguishing BJTs from unipolar FETs.

Conventional Current (I)

Current vectors point from positive (+) to negative (−). Negative electrons physically drift in the opposite direction.

Forward & Reverse Bias

Forward bias lowers the junction potential barrier (≈0.7 V for Si); reverse bias widens the barrier, blocking majority flow.

NPN vs. PNP Symbols & Emitter Arrow

Notice that only the arrow on the Emitter changes between the two complementary transistor types!

NPN Transistor "Not Pointing iN"
  • • Arrow points OUT (Not Pointing iN).
  • • Turn-on: V_BE ≈ +0.70 V.
  • • Currents: I_B and I_C enter; I_E exits.
PNP Transistor "Pointing iN"
  • • Arrow points IN (Points iN Proudly).
  • • Turn-on: V_EB ≈ +0.70 V.
  • • Currents: I_E enters; I_B and I_C exit.
💡 Memory Trick: The Emitter arrow points from P-type to N-type silicon—the exact path conventional current follows.

⚠️ Core Rule #1

Transistors Do NOT Create Energy!

When 0.05 mA base current unleashes 5.0 mA collector current, the transistor creates zero energy out of nothing. It acts as a variable throttle controlling energy delivered by the external power supply (V_CC).

📐 Core Rule #2

Gain (β) Is an Approximation

Beta varies with temperature, collector current, and manufacturing tolerances (two 2N3904 transistors may have β = 100 vs β = 250). In Saturation, β × I_B no longer applies as external circuit resistors cap the current.

Step 1 of 5

Base–Emitter junction is not forward-biased (V_BE < 0.7 V). A wide depletion barrier prevents carrier movement, so I_B = 0 and I_C ≈ 0.

🔄 What Changes in a PNP?

In PNP transistors, polarities and carriers reverse: positive holes [+] are emitted, Base voltage is held 0.7 V lower than Emitter, and current flows into Emitter and out of Base and Collector.

Section 04 • Practical Engineering

4. Practical BJT Applications & Worked Examples

Learn the four industry-standard BJT topologies: low-side LED switching, relay inductive flyback suppression, linear common-emitter amplification, and analog sensor thresholding.

APPLICATION A • DIGITAL SWITCH Cutoff = OFF | Saturation = ON

A. Low-Side LED Switch

A 3.3 V microcontroller pin delivers a tiny current to drive the BJT between Cutoff (V_in = 0 V) and Saturation (V_in = 3.3 V), turning the LED ON with minimal heat dissipation (V_CE ≈ 0.2 V).

STATE: SATURATION (SWITCH ON — LED LIT)
R_LED = (V_CC − V_F − V_CE(sat)) / I_LED = (5.0 − 2.0 − 0.2) / 0.020 = 140 Ω
R_B = (V_in − V_BE) / I_B(overdrive) = (3.3 − 0.7) / 0.0006 = 4.3 kΩ
APPLICATION B • INDUCTIVE DRIVER Flyback Diode Clamping

B. Relay / Motor Driver (Flyback Diode)

When an inductive coil turns OFF, the collapsing magnetic field causes an inductive spike V = -L(dI/dt). A parallel flyback diode safely recirculates this current, preventing BJT breakdown.

COIL ENERGIZED (IC = 98 mA)
I_C = (12.0 − 0.2) / 120 Ω = 98.3 mA
R_B = (5.0 − 0.7) / 0.002 = 2.15 kΩ (standard 2.2 kΩ)
APPLICATION C • LINEAR AMPLIFIER Active Mode | 180° Inversion

C. Common-Emitter Voltage Amplifier

Biasing cleanly in the Active region (V_CEQ ≈ ½V_CC) enables linear amplification of AC signals. The output waveform is 180° inverted relative to the base input.

A_v ≈ − R_C / R_E = − 4700 Ω / 470 Ω = −10 V/V
Why Inverted? V_C = V_CC − I_C·R_C. Higher v_in → Higher I_C → Lower V_C!
APPLICATION D • SENSOR THRESHOLD 0.7 V Trigger Level

D. Automatic Sensor Trigger (LDR)

A light-dependent resistor (LDR) in a voltage divider automatically triggers the BJT switch ON when ambient light drops below threshold.

V_th = V_CC × R_LDR / (R_top + R_LDR)
When V_th ≥ 0.70 V, Base current flows and activates the load!

BJT Pocket Cheat-Sheet

1. Arrow Mnemonics

• NPN = Not Pointing iN (Points OUT on Emitter).

• PNP = Points iN Proudly (Points IN on Emitter).

2. Governing Formulas
• I_E = I_B + I_C
• I_C = β × I_B (Active Mode)
• α = β / (β + 1) ≈ 0.99
• V_BE(on) ≈ 0.70 V (Silicon)
• V_CE(sat) ≈ 0.20 V (Saturation)
• P_D = V_CE × I_C (Thermal SOA ≤ 500mW)
• Stability S(I_CO) = (1+β) / [1+β·R_E/(R_E+R_TH)]
